Title: Quantum Biophysical Semeiotics: Osteocalcin Testing for Primary Prevention of Diabetes Mellitus
Authors: Sergio Stagnaro
Year: 2008
Paper ID: web_593585

Venue: clicMedicina, Genova, Anno VI - n°32

Abstract:
Traditional methods for diabetes screening are often costly, limited in scope, and ineffective for the early identification of individuals at congenital risk for diabetes mellitus. Quantum Biophysical Semeiotics (QBS) introduces a novel clinical approach that utilizes osteocalcin testing to detect and quantify both congenital predisposition and early-stage diabetes. This method involves the bedside application of physical stimulation to bone segments, followed by observation of microcirculatory and pancreatic responses, thereby evaluating the stimulation of insulin secretion by endogenous osteocalcin. The osteocalcin test is rapid, non-invasive, and can be performed at the bedside, offering a practical solution for early detection and primary prevention of diabetes. By enabling the identification of at-risk individuals before the onset of clinical symptoms, this approach has the potential to improve preventive strategies and reduce the incidence and complications associated with diabetes mellitus.
