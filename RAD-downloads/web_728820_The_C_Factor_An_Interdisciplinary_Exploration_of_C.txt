Abstract:
This multidisciplinary work investigates the intricate relationships among chaos, consciousness, knowledge, and comprehension, collectively referred to as the "C Factor." Through a series of thematic chapters, the authors synthesize scientific theories—including chaos theory, quantum mechanics, and Bohmian mechanics—with philosophical and metaphorical reflections on human understanding and values. The text delves into topics such as quantum potential, non-local reality, bio-physical semiotics, and the dynamic interplay between order and complexity. By integrating these diverse perspectives, the work offers a holistic view of complexity in both nature and human experience, ultimately addressing themes of creativity, inspiration, and the geometrization of complex systems. This essayistic and interdisciplinary synthesis aims to deepen our comprehension of the fundamental forces shaping reality and human cognition.
