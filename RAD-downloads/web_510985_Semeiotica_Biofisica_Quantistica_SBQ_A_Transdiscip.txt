Abstract:
Semeiotica Biofisica Quantistica (SBQ) represents a transdisciplinary approach in medicine, pioneered by Dr. Sergio Stagnaro, that focuses on the early diagnosis and prevention of diseases, including those in pre-clinical stages. SBQ is grounded in the interpretation of bodily signals—such as oscillations and vibrations—as a diagnostic language, integrating scientific rigor with a philosophical perspective centered on knowledge and compassion. The methodology employs a hypothetico-deductive scientific process inspired by Galileo, combining clinical observation, measurement of physiological oscillations, and experimental validation. SBQ has gained international recognition, particularly for its application in millimeter wave therapy targeting degenerative and mitochondrial diseases. Its efficacy is supported by clinical and experimental evidence, and the paradigm is disseminated through seminars, courses, and international conferences. The ongoing legacy of Dr. Stagnaro is reflected in the global adoption of SBQ, its integration into medical education, and its emphasis on uniting scientific knowledge with humanistic values in healthcare.
