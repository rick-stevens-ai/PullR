Title: Kidney Disease Specialist Josephine Briggs Appointed to Lead National Center for Complementary and Alternative Medicine

Abstract:
Josephine Briggs, a renowned expert in kidney disease with a demonstrated interest in complementary and alternative therapies, has been appointed as the new head of the National Center for Complementary and Alternative Medicine in Bethesda, Maryland. Previously, Briggs managed research grants at the National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases, where she initiated studies on the efficacy of alternative treatments such as cranberry juice for urinary tract infections and saw palmetto for enlarged prostate glands. Her appointment signals an increasing recognition of the importance of integrating non-traditional therapies into mainstream medical research and highlights the growing emphasis on evidence-based evaluation of complementary and alternative medicine.
