Title: Wee1 controls genomic stability during replication by regulating the Mus81-Eme1 endonuclease
Authors: Raquel Domínguez-Kelly, Yusé Martín, S. Koundrioukoff, M. Tanenbaum, V. Smits et al. (and 3 more)
Year: 2011
Paper ID: cd53a84be9dec802412e20e04f0f9733c289d838
URL: https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cd53a84be9dec802412e20e04f0f9733c289d838
Venue: Journal of Cell Biology

Abstract:
Wee1 is essential for normal DNA replication and for genomic stability, at least in part by inhibiting a general DNA damage response induced by the Mus81-Eme1 endonuclease.
