Title: DNA damage signaling in response to double-strand breaks during mitosis
Authors: Simona Giunta, R. Belotserkovskaya, S. Jackson
Year: 2010
Paper ID: 4c1ce383ea9ed97a883e337691cf5632e9e40365
URL: https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4c1ce383ea9ed97a883e337691cf5632e9e40365

Abstract:
Dividing cells can sense DNA damage and initiate a primary response, but repair isn’t completed until the cells enter G1.
